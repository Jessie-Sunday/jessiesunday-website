## The Release Series generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape portfolio project photograph, 1536x1024. Primary request: conceptual imagery for The Release Series, a women's wellness retreat centered on real connection. Scene: an intimate California countryside retreat, a long linen-covered communal table on a terrace surrounded by mature oak trees and golden grasses. Small group of adult women in casual natural fabrics chatting at the far end, candid unposed moments, ceramic cups, simple garden flowers and cushions. Style: editorial film photography, warm late-afternoon light, natural textures, subtle grain, relaxed and inviting. Composition: wide environmental shot with room around the scene for cropping. No text, no logos, no watermark. This is an invented atmosphere, not documentation of an actual event.
