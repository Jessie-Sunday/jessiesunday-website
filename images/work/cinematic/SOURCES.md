# Cinematic Selected Work photographs

Created November 7, 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool, using each original image in `../` as the edit target and the user's attached `the-release-series.webp` as the lighting/color reference. These variants are used by the Selected Work cards, mid-story case-study photographs, and neighbouring-project previews via `image.cinematicSrc`. Originals and illustrated case-study hero assets remain separate.

Treatment: warm amber light, soft cream highlights, open warm shadows, restrained greens, natural skin tones and subtle film texture. Generated edits may reinterpret small details; these are editorial variants of the credited source photography, not additional documentation. Original source URLs and ownership notes are in `../SOURCES.md`.

`the-release-series.webp` is a byte-for-byte copy of the user-supplied reference; no regeneration was needed. All final assets in this folder are WebP, up to 1600 pixels wide.
