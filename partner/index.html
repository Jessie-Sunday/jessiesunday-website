Good questions

Before we talk.

What does Jessie Sunday do?

I’m a San Francisco community and experience strategist. I help companies whose culture is their edge turn that culture into something people can feel: retreats, launches, brand activations, and community programs, from the first strategy conversation through production.

What is a fractional Head of Community and Experience?

It’s an embedded, ongoing role without a full-time hire. I join your team, hold the long-term direction, build a rhythm of gatherings people actually look forward to, and keep everyone pulling toward the same culture.

What’s the difference between fractional and project-based work?

Fractional and retainer work builds community over time, with me as a strategic partner inside your team. Project-based work brings one retreat, launch, or gathering from first sketch to a full room: we shape the ambition, agree the scope, and I bring the crew.

What kinds of experiences do you create?

Retreats and team gatherings, brand activations and summits, and community and third-space experiences. Past work includes pop-up haircut experiences in Dolores Park, a sold-out retreat for 60 women, and one shared culture for 500 ambassadors across 15 markets.

Who do you work with?

Values-led brands and founders, experiential and event agencies, visionary experience-led companies, and lifestyle, hospitality, and community brands.

Do you handle production, or only strategy?

Both. I own the why, then bring the crew for the how: producers, artists, technical teams, and local collaborators, pulled in around one clear brief. I stay in the room from the planning table to the last dance.

Where do you work?

I’m rooted in San Francisco and work across the Bay Area, the West Coast, and beyond. I build with local culture and local partners, so an experience feels earned, never imported.

Do I need a finished brief to get in touch?

No. Bring the beginning of an idea and the people you want in the room, and we’ll work out the shape together.
