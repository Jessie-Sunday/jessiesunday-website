# Illustrated former work heroes

These six images are **edits of the exact photographs formerly used as the work-page hero backgrounds**, not newly composed scenes. They were made with the built-in imagegen tool on November 7, 2026. Original sources remain unchanged in `public/images/work/`; their provenance is recorded in that folder's `SOURCES.md`.

The site's About-page illustration was supplied as the second input for style only. The outputs retain the original subjects, arrangements, and framing. Image generation can reinterpret small details, lettering, and faces; these are artistic interpretations, not additional event documentation. The Release Series source is itself the previously commissioned retreat concept.

Images are stored as WebP. The browser converts the light paper to transparency and tints the ink with the light/dark theme. Scroll motion translates the entire composition together; it does not rearrange subjects.
