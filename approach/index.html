JESSIE SUNDAY / APPROACH

From the first conversation
to the last dance.

People first. Then the idea. Then every detail that makes it real. Here’s what happens between hello and the doors opening.

The path from hello to a full room

Here’s how we get there.

  1. 01

    COMMUNITY & CULTURE STRATEGY

    Find the reason to gather.

    I go deep with your team before anyone talks about venues. Who are we bringing together? What do they care about? What do you want to be different afterward? We give the gathering a job before we give it a look.

    What we leave with

    A shared brief: the people, the purpose, and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    EXPERIENCE & CONCEPT DESIGN

    Give the idea a world.

    The brief becomes a creative direction. The setting, the pace, the way people arrive, the thing they’ll do together that they couldn’t do alone. Every choice should feel unmistakably like you, and nothing like a generic event.

    What we leave with

    An experience concept and creative direction the whole team can build from.

  3. 03

    COLLABORATORS & PRODUCTION

    Call in the crew.

    Producers, artists, technical teams, and local collaborators, pulled in around one clear idea. I stay in the room from the planning table to the last dance, so the brief never gets lost in the logistics.

    What we leave with

    An aligned crew, a production plan, and a room that feels the way we said it would.
